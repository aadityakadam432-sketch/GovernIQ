GovernIQ ✦

A lightweight, browser-based Brand Governance & Content Compliance Platform designed to ensure marketing copy, advertisements, and digital assets align with brand guidelines before publishing.

🌟 Key Features

Rule-Based Content Reviewer: Instantly checks copy against configurable brand guidelines, phrase matches, and restricted terminology.

Claims & Evidence Detection: Flags absolute or unsupported marketing claims (e.g., guaranteed success, best ever, or numerical metric claims) requiring human verification.

Governance Controls & Visual Inspection: Upload approved visual asset references and inspect upcoming artwork against registered brand color palettes.

Accessibility Quick Checks: Automated color contrast calculations against WCAG standards (calculating luminance ratios for text and background combinations).

Role & Permission Demonstration: Preview workflows tailored for Creators, Managers, and Authorised Reviewers.

Local-First Architecture: Operates entirely in the browser using localStorage. No external AI APIs or data tracking required out-of-the-box.

Analytics & Audit Logs: Track compliance metrics, flagged categories, and export audit history to JSON for reporting.
